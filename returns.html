Returns & Exchanges

Last Updated: February 4, 2026

30-Day Money Back Guarantee

We stand behind our products. If you're not completely satisfied with your DIXX, return them within 30 days for a full refund or exchange.

1. Return Eligibility

To be eligible for a return, items must:

  • Be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
  • Have all original tags attached
  • Be in original packaging
  • Be returned within 30 days of delivery

Note: For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on opened or worn underwear unless defective.

2. How to Return

  1. Contact Us: Email returns@dixxco.com or use our contact form
  2. Get Authorization: We'll send you a return authorization number and instructions
  3. Pack Your Items: Include the return authorization number in your package
  4. Ship It Back: Use the prepaid return label we provide (US only)

3. Exchanges

Need a different size or color? We offer free exchanges!

  • Contact us within 30 days of delivery
  • We'll send you the new item and a prepaid return label
  • Send back the original item within 14 days

4. Refund Processing

Once we receive your return:

  • We'll inspect the items within 2-3 business days
  • Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
  • Refunds are issued to your original payment method
  • You'll receive an email confirmation when your refund is processed

5. Return Shipping

US Customers

We provide a prepaid return shipping label. Return shipping is FREE for exchanges and defective items. For refunds, a $5.99 return shipping fee will be deducted from your refund.

International Customers

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using a trackable shipping method.

6. Defective or Damaged Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • Provide photos of the defect or damage
  • We'll send a replacement immediately at no cost
  • Return shipping is FREE for defective items

7. Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Gift cards
  • Final sale items (marked as such at checkout)
  • Items returned after 30 days

8. Questions?

Have questions about returns or exchanges? We're here to help!
