Shipping Policy

Last Updated: February 4, 2026

1. Shipping Options

Standard Shipping (5-7 Business Days)

  • FREE on orders over $50
  • $5.99 flat rate on orders under $50

Express Shipping (2-3 Business Days)

  • $12.99 flat rate

Overnight Shipping (1 Business Day)

  • $24.99 flat rate
  • Orders must be placed before 2 PM EST

2. Processing Time

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or holidays will be processed the next business day.

3. Shipping Destinations

We currently ship to all 50 US states and select international destinations. International shipping rates and times vary by location.

International Shipping

  • Canada: 7-14 business days
  • UK & Europe: 10-21 business days
  • Australia: 14-28 business days

Note: International customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

4. Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your package at any time through the carrier's website.

5. Shipping Restrictions

We cannot ship to P.O. boxes for express or overnight shipping. Standard shipping to P.O. boxes is available.

6. Lost or Damaged Packages

If your package is lost or arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of the expected delivery date. We'll work with the carrier to resolve the issue or send a replacement.

7. Address Changes

If you need to change your shipping address, please contact us immediately. Once an order has shipped, we cannot modify the delivery address.
